Who We Are

Strength You Can
Count On

Based in the heart of Siyaganj, Indore, Malik Enterprise has been a cornerstone of quality rope and niwar supply across Madhya Pradesh. We deal in the full spectrum of fibrous materials — from rugged industrial ropes to traditional weaving niwars.

Whether you need bulk synthetic rope, fine Jari, or vibrant Resham ropes — we have the inventory and expertise to deliver exactly what your project demands.
